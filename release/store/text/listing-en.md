# Don't Trust the Door — listing (English)

## Title
Don't Trust the Door

## Short description (138 characters, max 160)
A cute troll platformer: 20 levels, 241 traps. The floor, the coins, the springs and even the EXIT door lie to you. Die, laugh, try again.

## Long description
Don't Trust the Door is a cute troll platformer where the level itself betrays you.

The floor gives way, "safe" coins bite, springs launch you into spikes, the ceiling comes down, your controls swap, gravity flips, the EXIT door runs away and the camera lies. Later levels bring buttons that do the opposite of what they promise, crates you push and climb, water you swim in, keys and locks, lasers, shrinking and growing, wind, ghosts, fireworks and signs that send you the wrong way.

There are 20 hand-made levels with 241 traps, and every trap is a different idea. Each one surprises you the first time and can be beaten the second time: the hero is always on screen and every trap has a fair solution. Each level is longer than the one before and has one more trap.

Death costs nothing: you restart at once, from the last checkpoint flag. A death counter keeps score. Stuck? Each level has one hint that you choose when to use. Your progress is saved.

Play with the keyboard or with the on-screen touch buttons. Available in 8 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian and Turkish.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** Move: Left / Right arrows or A / D. Jump: Up arrow, W, Space, Z or K. Restart the level: R. Pause: P. Sound on / off: M. Next / confirm: Enter.
- **Touch:** Move: the ◀ and ▶ buttons at the bottom left. Jump: the ▲ button at the bottom right (hold for a higher jump). On-screen buttons for restart, pause, sound and the hint (bulb).
